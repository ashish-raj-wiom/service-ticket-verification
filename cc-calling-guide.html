Early Close Calling Guide

Some tickets are marked resolved while the customer’s router is still offline. These calls are to find out why the CSP closes a ticket before it is fixed. This guide shows what the first calls found, and how to make the calls.

01

Why we are calling

When a CSP or technician marks a ticket resolved, Wiom closes it and tells the customer it is fixed. In about one in three closures, Wiom’s data shows the customer’s router still offline at that moment, and those customers come back 3.4 times as often as the rest.

The goal of these calls is to understand why the CSP closes the ticket while the router is still offline. Every call ends with one of four answers: the customer actually has internet (a problem in Wiom’s data); the CSP told the customer when it would be fixed; the CSP closed it without telling the customer, and we ask the CSP why; or the customer did not answer, and we ask the CSP what the issue is.

20 tickets marked resolved while the customer’s router was offline. The CSP was called first, and the customer in three cases.

Most of these were early closes. The CSP marked the ticket resolved, and the internet came back hours later. Meanwhile the customer had been told it was fixed.

03

The calling process

Each call answers one question: why did the CSP close this ticket while the customer’s router was offline? Start with the customer, and call the CSP when the customer’s answer calls for it.

Step by step

StepWhat you do
1 · Pick the ticketTake the next row on the call sheet. Every row is a ticket marked resolved while the customer’s router was offline, and still offline when the sheet was made. If the sheet now shows the router back online, record BACK-BEFORE-CALL and move on
2 · Call the customerAsk whether their internet is working now. Two tries, between 11:00 and 21:00
3a · Internet workingThe customer has internet but Wiom shows the router offline, so the problem is in Wiom’s data. Record NET-WORKING and report the ticket to the IX team. No call to the CSP
3b · Internet not workingAsk whether the CSP called them and said when it would be fixed
4a · CSP gave a timeRecord what was promised and by when. Record COMMITTED
4b · CSP did not callRecord NO-CALL. Call the CSP and ask why the ticket was closed before it was fixed. Then follow the CSP’s answer below
5a · “It shows green on my portal”Ask for a screenshot of their portal. Report the ticket to the IX team with the screenshot. Reason code R-PORTAL
5b · “I am fixing it”Ask why they marked it resolved before it was fixed. Record their answer in their own words and pick the closest reason code
5c · Any other answerRecord it in their own words and pick the closest reason code
3c · Customer did not answerRecord C-DNP. Call the CSP and ask what the issue is. Record what they say and pick the closest reason code. Try the customer again later

Reporting to the IX team

Send the IX team the Kapture ticket ID, the time you called, what the customer or CSP said, and the CSP’s portal screenshot if you have one. These are cases where Wiom’s data and what people see on the ground disagree, and the IX team needs to find out why.

What to say

WhoAskListen for
Customer“Kya aapka internet abhi chal raha hai?” (Is your internet working now?)A clear yes or no. If they are unsure, ask them to open any website or video and tell you
Customer, if not working“Kya aapke CSP ne aapko call karke bataya ki internet kab tak theek ho jayega?” (Did your CSP call you and say when the internet would be fixed?) If yes: “Kab tak ka bola tha?” (By when did they say?)Yes or no, and the time they were given
CSP, if the customer was not told“Ticket [number] customer ka net abhi bhi band hai. Ticket theek hone se pehle close kyun hua?” (The customer’s internet on ticket [number] is still off. Why was the ticket closed before it was fixed?)Their reason in their own words
CSP, if they say “I am fixing it”“Theek ho raha hai toh resolve pehle kyun kiya?” (If it is still being fixed, why was it resolved already?)Why they closed early: time, rating, someone else closed it, or something else
CSP, if the portal shows green“Kya aap apne portal ka screenshot bhej sakte hain?” (Can you send a screenshot of your portal?)The screenshot, for the IX team
CSP, if the customer did not answer“Ticket [number] customer se baat nahi ho payi, aur router abhi bhi offline dikh raha hai. Kya issue hai?” (We couldn’t reach the customer on ticket [number], and the router still shows offline. What is the issue?)What the fault is, and whether it is fixed
Keep it neutral with the CSP. We are finding out why tickets close early, not blaming anyone.
05

How to record it

Pick one outcome code for the Status column. If you spoke to the CSP, also pick the closest reason code, and write what was said in Remarks.

Outcome

CodeUse when
BACK-BEFORE-CALLThe sheet shows the router back online before you called
NET-WORKINGThe customer says the internet works; reported to the IX team
COMMITTEDNot working; the CSP had told the customer when it would be fixed
NO-CALLNot working; the CSP had not told the customer anything
C-DNPThe customer did not pick up
CSP-DNPYou needed the CSP but they did not pick up

The CSP’s reason

CodeThe CSP says
R-PORTALTheir own portal or OLT shows the customer online (green); reported to the IX team
R-TIMEThey closed to stay within time, or to protect their rating
R-SOMEONE-ELSESomeone else closed it: the owner, a technician, or they don’t know who
R-BLOCKERSomething outside their control: road works, area outage, power, home locked
R-OTHERAny other reason; write it in Remarks
06

Rules to follow

  • Never tell a customer their internet is fixed unless they tell you it works.
  • Silence is not a yes. A customer who doesn’t pick up has not confirmed anything.
  • Call only between 11:00 and 21:00.
  • Write down what people actually said. Don’t record how they sounded.
  • The sheet has customer phone numbers and addresses. Keep it inside the team. Don’t forward or download it.
  • The sheet’s router data runs about 2 hours behind. A very recent fix may not show yet.
