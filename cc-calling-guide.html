Early Close Calling Guide

Some tickets are marked resolved while the customer’s internet is still off. This guide shows what our first calls found, and how to check these tickets by phone every day until the system does it automatically.

01

Why we are calling

When a CSP or technician marks a ticket resolved, Wiom closes it and tells the customer it is fixed. Wiom’s own data already shows whether the customer’s router is online at that moment, but today nothing acts on it.

In about one in three closures, the router is still offline when the ticket is closed. Those customers come back 3.4 times as often as the rest. Until the system checks this by itself, we check these tickets by phone.

02

What the first calls found

20 tickets marked resolved while the customer’s router was offline. The CSP was called first, and the customer in three cases.

Most of these were early closes. The CSP marked the ticket resolved, and the internet came back hours later. Meanwhile the customer had been told it was fixed.

03

The daily routine

StepWhenWhat happens
1 · Get the list10:30A call sheet is shared every morning. It lists yesterday’s and this morning’s tickets that were marked resolved while the router was offline and are still offline. A quarter of the rows are marked “do not call”: leave those alone, they show whether calling makes a difference
2 · Check before callingBefore each callIf the sheet now shows the router back online, record BACK-BEFORE-CALL and move on
3 · Call the CSP11:00–21:00Ask what was done and why it was closed. See the script below
4 · Call the customer11:00–21:00, 2 triesAsk one yes-or-no question. See the script below
5 · ActSame dayFollow the table in “What to do with each answer”
6 · RecordAfter each callOne outcome code in Status, and notes in Remarks

Order of calling: “recharge done, no internet” tickets first, because the customer has already paid; then CSPs with several tickets on the list; then the longest offline.

What to say

WhoAskListen for
CSP“Ticket [number] aapne resolve kiya tha. Uss customer ke yaha kya kaam hua tha, aur ticket kab aur kisne close kiya?” (You resolved ticket [number]. What was done at this customer’s home, and who closed the ticket, and when?)What was fixed, who closed it, and whether they checked with the customer. If they say their portal shows the customer online, ask for a screenshot and a photo of the label on the Wiom router
Customer“Kya aapka internet abhi chal raha hai?” (Is your internet working now?)A clear yes or no. If they are unsure, ask them to open any website or video and tell you
Keep it neutral with the CSP. We are finding out what happened, not blaming anyone.

06

How to record it

Pick one code for the Status column. Write what was said in Remarks, in the person’s own words where you can.

CodeUse when
BACK-BEFORE-CALLThe router came back online before you called
C-YESThe customer says the internet works
C-NOThe customer says it does not work; you reopened the ticket
C-DNPThe customer did not pick up
CSP-DNPThe CSP did not pick up
EARLY-CLOSEThe CSP says they closed before fixing it, and fixed it later
BLOCKERSomething outside the CSP’s control: road works, area outage, home locked or power off
PORTAL-UPThe CSP closed because their own portal showed the customer online
ISP-PLANThe customer’s ISP plan or recharge was the problem, not the line
07

Rules to follow

  • Never tell a customer their internet is fixed unless they tell you it works.
  • Silence is not a yes. A customer who doesn’t pick up has not confirmed anything.
  • Call only between 11:00 and 21:00.
  • A reopened ticket is the same issue. Always tell the CSP so.
  • Record the answer as said: yes, no, or not reached. Don’t record how someone sounded.
  • The sheet has customer phone numbers and addresses. Keep it inside the team. Don’t forward or download it.
  • The sheet’s router data runs about 2 hours behind. A very recent fix may not show yet.
